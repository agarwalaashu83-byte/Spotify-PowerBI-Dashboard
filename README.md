# Spotify-PowerBI-Dashboard
Spotify Streaming Performance Dashboard
Power BI | Interactive Data Visualization

Objective
Analyze streaming performance across 500 global artists to uncover patterns in success by demographic, geography, genre, and collaboration style — and present findings in a decision-ready interactive dashboard.

Dataset
500 artists with 14 attributes: total/lead/feature/solo/collaborative streams, artist type (solo/group), sex, country of origin, primary language, primary genre, and debut year. ~8.41M total streams across the dataset.

Tools & Skills
Power BI (DAX measures, interactive slicers, KPI cards), data cleaning, exploratory analysis, dashboard design, data storytelling.

Approach

Built 4 KPI cards (Total Streams, Total Artists, Average Streams, Top Artist) for at-a-glance context
Designed 6 visuals covering artist type, country, gender, genre, debut year trend, and stream type (solo vs. collaborative)
Added cross-filtering slicers (country, language, genre, sex) for user-driven exploration
Distilled findings into a single written insight callout to guide interpretation

Key Insights

Solo artists generate ~80% of total streams despite being less numerous than expected relative to groups
Male artists account for 76.6% of streams vs. 19.5% female — a notable representation gap worth further investigation
The United States alone contributes 57% of total streams (4.79M of 8.41M), reflecting a highly concentrated market
Hip-Hop and Pop are the dominant genres both by artist count and total streams
Streaming activity is concentrated among artists who debuted in the 2000s–2010s
